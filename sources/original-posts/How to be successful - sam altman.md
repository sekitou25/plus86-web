# Reading how to be successful by Sam Altman

链接 https://blog.samaltman.com/how-to-be-successful

1. **Compound yourself** 复利
	1. Exponential curves are the key to wealth generation.
	2. 关注增速，关注斜率。因为指数级增长非常重要
	3. leverage 非常关键，目的是让单位的工作得到更多的结果。
		1. There are many ways to get this leverage, such as capital, technology, brand, network effects, and managing people.
		2. 有 leverage 的前提是，有值得放大的东西。先变强，再放大。
	4. adding another zero to whatever you define as your success metric
	5. 相信 step 方式的跃进，会有阵痛，但相信这样的前进方式。
2. **Have almost too much self-belief**
	1. Managing your own morale—and your team’s morale—is one of the greatest challenges of most endeavors.
	2. 自我相信非常重要，因为达到目标道阻且长。要有着几乎是自我催眠的那种自信，要 certainty
3. **Learn to think independently**
	1. 第一性原理
	2. ==“I will fail many times, and I will be really right once” ==
4. **Get good at “sales”**
	1. you also have to be able to convince other people of what you believe.
	2. My best advice for communicating clearly is to first make sure your thinking is clear and then use plain, concise language.
	3. The best way to be good at sales is to genuinely believe in what you’re selling.
	4. face to face
5. **Make it easy to take risks**
	1. Most people overestimate risk and underestimate reward.
		1. 这一点非常重要，特别是做交易，更是如此。我们都是用小的可能损失，去换取更大的利润。
	2. Look for small bets you can make where you lose 1x if you’re wrong but make 100x if it works. Then make a bigger bet in that direction.
	3. Keeping your life cheap and flexible for as long as you can is a powerful way to do this
		1. 是这样的，我们讨论过。创业最好的路径机会住一个能睡觉和洗澡的地方，然后所有的时间都放在事业上。但是 tradeoff 也很明显。我做不到这一点，但我可以尽可能的做。
6. **Focus**
	1. Once you have figured out what to do, be unstoppable about getting your small handful of priorities accomplished quickly.
7. **Work hard**
	1. **Extreme people get extreme results**
	2. 前提满足前面的要素，比如要做能拿到 leverage 的工作，要做指数增长的工作。钢铁厂里面的工人努力工作并不会拿到好的回报
	3. work stamina seems to be one of the biggest predictors of long-term success.
	4. 要有长期的耐力
8. **Be bold**
	1. I believe that it’s easier to do a hard startup than an easy startup. People want to be part of something exciting and feel that their work matters.
		1. 感觉难的事情，和感觉简单的事情。真正做起来是一样难的。所以就去做一个有想象空间的事情
	2. Things that seem exciting to you will often seem exciting to other people too.
9. **Be willful**
	1. People have an enormous capacity to make things happen. ==A combination of self-doubt, giving up too early, and not pushing hard enough prevents most people from ever reaching anywhere near their potential.==
	2. willful 可以让我们坚持的更久，久到 luck go our way
10. **Be hard to compete with**
	1. The best way to become difficult to compete with is to build up leverage.
		1. 怎么做呢
			1. 单一技能容易被替代，但两三个技能的交叉点很难被复制。
			2. 积累有复利效应的资产。作品、品牌、networking。这一项护城河的本质是时间
			3. 把自己放在"正和博弈"的位置。产生价值，世界就会放大影响力
	2. ==你的网络质量决定了你能接触到什么级别的机会。要主动的向上 reach out==
11. **Build a network**
	1. An effective way to build a network is to help people as much as you can.
		1. 这一点也深有感触。在以前的工作中也是，越「老好人」的做一些事情，其实会在更长期带来回报。
	2. One of the best ways to build a network is to develop a reputation for really taking care of the people who work with you
12. **You get rich by owning things**
	1. You get truly rich by owning things that increase rapidly in value.
	2. The best way to make things that increase rapidly in value is by making things people want at scale.
		1. 什么意思呢？找到一个真实的需求，然后用一种边际成本趋近于零的方式去满足它。这就是价值快速增长的公式。
13. **Be internally driven**
	1. 很好理解。但这一点更重要的是，这是避免人生陷入无聊的关键。
	2. 摘录下来吧
	3. ==The most successful people I know are primarily internally driven; they do what they do to impress themselves and because they feel compelled to make something happen in the world. After you’ve made enough money to buy whatever you want and gotten enough social status that it stops being fun to get more, this is the only force I know of that will continue to drive you to higher levels of performance.==
